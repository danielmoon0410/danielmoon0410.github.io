Background
From Calcutta to Seoul

Born and raised in India, I moved to South Korea to pursue Electrical and Computer Engineering at Seoul National University. This cross-cultural journey shaped my ability to adapt quickly and collaborate across diverse environments—skills I carry into both research and teamwork.

My work sits at the intersection of AI models and the hardware that runs them. Through hands-on experience with FPGA boards, NPU architectures, and quantization frameworks, I've developed a system-level perspective: understanding not just how neural networks are designed, but how they execute on real silicon under power and area constraints.
